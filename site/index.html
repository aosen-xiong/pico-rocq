Proof Organization

  • Subtyping.v defines qualifier, class, and qualified-type subtyping.
  • PreservationCall.v holds the method-call case of preservation.
  • ReadonlyFlat.v and ReadonlyWorlds.v prove readonly-state preservation without type parameters.
  • ArgTheorems.v ends the general readonly-state and transitive-state proof.
  • The PICO mechanization without generics is kept in the PICO/ folder of the repository.
